## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Issue: Snake Animation Not Showing

**Solution:**
1. Verify you completed Step 1 (Enable Actions)
2. Verify workflow completed successfully (check Actions tab)
3. Wait 1-2 minutes after workflow completion
4. Hard refresh: `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (Mac)

### Issue: GitHub Stats Not Loading

**Solution:**
1. Wait 2-3 minutes for external services to initialize
2. Hard refresh the page
3. Check Vercel services status (external dependencies)
4. Verify username `hemantrajput077` is correct

### Issue: Profile View Counter Not Working

**Note:** This is normal
- Counter only increments for unique visitors
- Your own visits don't count
- May take a few hours to start working
